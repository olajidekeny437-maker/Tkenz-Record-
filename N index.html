About Us

Tkenz Record is a Nigerian music and entertainment brand dedicated to discovering, developing, and promoting talented artists and creating music that connects with people across Africa and beyond.

We believe music is more than entertainment. It is a way to tell stories, express emotions, inspire people, and bring different cultures together. Our goal is to build a platform where creativity, talent, and originality can grow.

Our Story

Tkenz is built around the journey of twins Taiye and Kehinde, who perform together as Tkenz. They hail from Ile-Ife, Osun State, Nigeria, and were born and raised in Jos, Plateau State.

Their educational journey began at G.S.S. Garaku in Kokona L.G.A., Nasarawa State, before they moved to Lagos to pursue their dreams and develop their passion for music.

From their early experiences to their journey in music, Tkenz represents determination, creativity, and the belief that with passion and consistency, dreams can become reality.

Today, Tkenz Record is focused on creating quality music, supporting artistic talent, and building a lasting entertainment brand from Nigeria to the world.

Our Vision

To build a respected African music and entertainment brand that gives talented artists the opportunity to be heard, discovered, and connected with audiences around the world.

Our Mission

To create, promote, and support great music while providing artists with a platform to develop their talents and reach new audiences.
