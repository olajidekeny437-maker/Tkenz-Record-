About Us

Get to know Tkenz Record and what we stand for.

Who We Are

Tkenz Record is a music and entertainment brand built around creativity, quality music and the development of talented artists.

We are passionate about creating music that connects with people while building a platform where artists can grow and express themselves.

Our Story

The journey behind Tkenz and Tkenz Record.

Where It Started

The story of Tkenz began with twins Taiye and Kehinde, who perform together as Tkenz. They hail from Ile-Ife, Osun State, Nigeria, and were born and raised in Jos, Plateau State.

The Journey

Their educational journey began at G.S.S. Garaku in Kokona L.G.A., Nasarawa State. From there, their journey continued to Lagos as they pursued their dreams and developed their passion for music.

Through music, Tkenz seeks to connect with people, tell meaningful stories and create songs that can be enjoyed both in Nigeria and around the world.

The Birth of Tkenz Record

Tkenz Record represents the next step in that journey — creating opportunities, supporting creativity and building a platform where music and talent can grow.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a respected African music and entertainment brand that discovers talent, develops artists and brings quality Nigerian music to audiences around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: create great music, support artists, encourage creativity and build lasting connections through entertainment.

Our Artists

Meet the talent behind the sound of Tkenz Record.

Tkenz

Tkenz is a musical duo made up of twins Taiye and Kehinde. Their sound draws inspiration from African music and modern Afro-fusion, creating songs that connect with listeners through melody, rhythm and storytelling.

With their unique identity and passion for music, Tkenz continues to develop their sound and reach new audiences.
