About Us

Tkenz Record is a music and entertainment brand focused on creating, developing and promoting talented artists and quality music.

Our goal is to build a platform where creativity, originality and passion can grow while connecting our music with listeners in Nigeria and around the world.

Our Story

Tkenz Record was created from a passion for music, creativity and the desire to give talented artists a platform to express themselves.

The journey behind Tkenz began with a love for music and entertainment. From writing songs and developing sounds to sharing music with listeners, every step has been part of a bigger vision.

Tkenz represents creativity, determination and the belief that great music can connect people across different backgrounds and locations.

Today, Tkenz Record continues to develop its identity as a growing music and entertainment brand, with a focus on artists, original music and meaningful entertainment experiences.

This is only the beginning. Our story is still being written, and we invite you to be part of the journey.
